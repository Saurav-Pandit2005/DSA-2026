** Introduction to Backtracking
---------------------------------

-> Backtracking in Java is a recursive problem-solving technique where you build solutions step by step, undoing choices when they lead to dead ends.

-> It’s widely used in puzzles (Sudoku, N-Queens), pathfinding (mazes), and generating combinations or permutations.

* What is Backtracking?
--------------------------

    -> Definition: Backtracking is a trial-and-error algorithm that explores possible solutions incrementally and abandons invalid paths early.

    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    * Core Idea:
    --------------

    -> Choose – Make a decision (e.g., place a queen, pick a number).

    -> Explore – Recursively continue with that choice.

    -> Check validity – If invalid, undo the choice.

    -> Repeat – Try other options until a valid solution is found or all possibilities are exhausted.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
