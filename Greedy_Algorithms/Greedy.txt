** Introduction to Greedy Apporch
----------------------------------

-> Greedy algorithms are a problem-solving strategy that builds solutions step by step, always choosing the option that looks best at the moment. 

->  They are widely used in optimization problems like shortest paths, minimum spanning trees, and data compression.

----------------------------------- OR ----------------------------------

-> Greedy Algorithms is a problem solving technique where we can make the locally optimum choice at each stage & hope to achieve a global optimum.


    * Pros 
    --------

    1) Simple & Easy
    2) Good Enough TC

    * Cons 
    -------

    1) A lot of time, global optimum is not achieved.
